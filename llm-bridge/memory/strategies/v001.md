# Strategy v001: Brutal opener

(starting strategy, written by hand; the master agent rewrites this after every match)

## Opening (minutes 0-6)
- Scout every enemy start area at once; re-scout when the scout comes home.
- Stance expand until 2 refineries are up, then harass.
- As soon as an enemy base is known, raid its miners with 3-5 fast vehicles.

## Middle game
- From minute 6, build one big mixed army and attack with it once it is worth more than $5,000.
- Target the enemy war factory first, then refineries, then the construction yard.
- Keep building while the wave fights, so the next wave follows quickly.

## Counters
- More than 3 enemy aircraft or drones seen: build antiair until they are gone.
- 5 or more enemy defences in a base: build artillery and target defences.
- Enemy army mostly infantry: build armour; mostly tanks: build armour and rocket infantry.

## When to attack, defend, retreat
- Enemy army heading toward our base and bigger than ours: defend.
- Wave lost twice what it killed and is below half its value: retreat.
- Big army at home, no threat, money banked: attack.
